# Home page demo (redesign)

Scope: artboxsocial.com home page, visual demo before the full rebuild. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Jaipur business owners and Indian D2C/lifestyle brands, deciding if Artbox can make their brand look good on a phone every week.
Action: enquire (WhatsApp, phone, email, short form). Proof: real client names and real testimonials only; case-study metrics stay out until confirmed.

Directions considered (by resonance): Instagram profile grid; Pink City facade windows; Sanganeri block-print workshop; contact sheet with grease-pencil selects (assigned, seed a52b137a); hand-painted signboards; shoot call sheet; the literal art box.

## Direction contract
seed: a52b137a

THESIS: The page is a photographer's contact sheet marked up in grease pencil. Artbox shoots, circles the frames that work and posts them. Refuses the agency default of headline + gradient hero + grid of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm film-black sheet, frames separated by rebate strips with edge-print frame numbers; dusty-rose china-marker circles, crosses and handwritten notes (Kalam); heavy expanded white caps (Archivo) as in the ARTBOX logo; a white light-table ground for quotes; a drenched dusty-rose ground for the services and the enquiry.

STORY: See sample frames being picked, understand Artbox makes content then runs the feed and plans it, see real clients and their words, enquire.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "SHOT. CIRCLED. POSTED." over a live contact sheet of sample frames, two already circled; WhatsApp and Start-a-project actions visible.

SIGNATURE: Visitor circles frames (content types); the circled set prefills the enquiry form.

RISK: Dark sheet may read moody for schools and gyms; rose sections and the light table carry the warmth. Frames are labelled samples, not client work.
