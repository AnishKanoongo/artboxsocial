## 2. What the data says

### 2.1 One viral Reel hides how small typical reach is
- The **29 May 2026** Reel *"Your brand without marketing is like posting one blurry story…"* reached **186,322** accounts, with 232K views, 5,988 shares and 1,954 saves. That is more reach than all the other 80 posts put together.
- Without it, a typical Reel reaches **about 1,450** accounts, a carousel about 1,190, and a single image about 560.
- **It was reposted on 5 Oct 2026 with the same caption and reached only 1,496.** Reposting a hit does not repeat it. The idea (a brand-pain analogy that is funny and painfully relatable) is what worked, so make new versions of that idea instead of re-uploading the video.

### 2.2 Format performance (all 81 posts)

| Format | Posts | Median reach | Avg saves | Avg shares |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels | 56 | 1,120 | 3.7* | 15.7* |
| Carousels | 17 | 583 | 9.6 | 23.8 |
| Single images | 8 | 513 | 1.1 | 5.5 |

\*Excludes the viral Reel.

- **Reels bring reach. Carousels bring saves, profile visits and follows.** The Instagram API does not report follows for Reels, but carousels drove **all 49 tracked follows** and 1,776 of the 1,798 tracked profile visits.
- **Stop posting single images.** They are the weakest format on every metric.

### 2.3 The first 3 seconds of a Reel decide its reach
How often viewers skip a Reel in the first 3 seconds tracks reach very closely:

| 3-second skip rate | Typical reach |
|---|---|
| Below 50% | **2,000 to 4,700** (and the 186K viral Reel was at 41%) |
| 50 to 60% | 1,000 to 2,000 |
| Above 60% | **100 to 1,200** |

The median skip rate across all Reels is **57%**, so more than half of viewers leave before the hook finishes. Improving the opening is the biggest lever available.

### 2.4 What topics work
**Top performers:**
- Agency-life memes: *"freedom from revisions"*, *"jaha client ka message aa jaye wahi office"*, *"brief becomes a little TOO creative"*. These reach 2.3K to 4.7K.
- Hiring and culture carousels: *"next camera person"* had 6.1K reach, 21 follows and 556 profile visits; *"if you're always coming up with ideas…"* had 4.3K reach and 549 profile visits.
- Trend-jacking (T20 World Cup, *Dhurandhar 2*, Independence Day) gets lots of shares.

**Low performers:**
- Plain self-promotion: *"We'll do whatever to make you our client"* (106 reach), *"We handle everything from cool content ideas…"* (360), *"Your brand doesn't need more content…"* (350).
- Motivational or "day" posts: Women's Day (271), *"never feel sorry for posting your business"* (130).
- Engagement bait with no hook: *"Drop your brand below…"* (116).

**The catch:** memes and hiring posts mostly reach **other marketers, creators and job-seekers**. Shares are high and comments are close to zero, and none of these posts prompts a business owner to message you. The content that converts clients (case studies, results, before/after) is almost missing from the feed.

### 2.5 Irregular posting is hurting reach
- Posts per month: Sep 2026 = **1**, Feb and Nov 2025 = **0**, Mar and Jul 2026 = 10.
- After the 10 Sep post, daily reach fell from about 400 to **50 to 150** between 21 Sep and 2 Oct, and follower reach dropped to single digits.
- On **5 Oct, three posts went out within 80 minutes.** The third reached only 106 accounts because the three posts competed with each other.

### 2.6 Timing (IST, median reach per post)
- **Sunday** (median about 3,000) and **Friday** (about 1,260) do best. Most posts go out Tue to Thu, which get 700 to 830.
- Most posts go out between **4 and 6 PM**, which gives middling results. The few posts at **7 to 8 PM** have the highest medians (2,000 to 3,000). **11 AM to 12 PM** is a reasonable second slot.
- The sample is small, so test these windows before treating them as fixed.

### 2.7 Audience quality is the hidden problem
- **Age:** 25 to 34 is the core group (54% of followers whose age is known), followed by 35 to 44 (16%). This matches founders and business owners.
- **Countries:** India 3,787 (about 55%), **Pakistan 1,090 (16%)**, Latvia 213, US 146, **Israel 140, Iraq 127**, UK 75.
- **Cities:** Jaipur 697, Mumbai 461, Delhi 442, **Karachi 315, Lahore 271, Riga 175**, Kolkata 114, Bangalore 123.
- Roughly **25 to 30% of followers come from places you don't serve** (Pakistan, Latvia, Iraq, Israel). This is typical of past follow-for-follow, giveaway or bought-follower activity. Following 1,732 accounts points the same way.
- These followers never engage, which drags down engagement rate. That partly explains why only **2 to 5% of followers see a typical post**.

## 3. Strategy

**Positioning:** *"The Jaipur agency that makes D2C and local brands grow, and shows you the receipts."*
Keep the humour, since it is your reach engine, but have every funny post lead to the same message: we get results for business owners.

### Content pillars and weekly mix (4 to 5 posts a week, at least 1 day apart)

| Pillar | Share | Format | Purpose | Example ideas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Brand-pain memes** | 30% | Reels | Reach | New versions of the viral idea: *"Your brand without a content calendar is like…"*, *"Client says 'make it viral' / budget says…"* |
| **2. Proof and case studies** | 25% | Carousels and Reels | Conversion | *"How we took @client from 0 to 10K in 90 days"*, before/after feed redesigns, screenshots of reel views, client video testimonials |
| **3. Quick lessons for owners** | 20% | Carousels | Saves and authority | *"5 hooks that stopped the scroll for our Jaipur café client"*, *"Why your reel dies in 3 seconds"* (use your own skip-rate data) |
| **4. Behind the scenes and culture** | 15% | Reels and carousels | Trust and hiring | Shoot-day vlogs, founder @snigdha_gupta on camera, hiring posts (your best follow drivers) |
| **5. Trend-jacks** | 10% | Reels | Shares | Cricket, Bollywood releases and festivals, always tied back to marketing |

**Stop:** single-image posts, "drop your brand below" posts, plain "hire us" posts, reposting old Reels, and posting more than once a day.

### Reel production rules (to get the 3-second skip rate under 50%)
1. Put the payoff or tension in the first frame, with on-screen text in the first second and no logo intro.
2. Keep Reels 7 to 15 seconds. Your best Reels hold viewers for 7 to 12 seconds on average.
3. Use Hinglish where it fits. *"Jaha client ka message aa jaye wahi office"* was a top-5 Reel.
4. Put the CTA in the caption or pinned comment, not in the video: *"DM 'GROW' for a free profile audit."*
5. Batch-shoot 8 to 10 Reels a month in one day so posting never stops.

### Profile and funnel fixes (do these this week)
- **Bio:** *"We grow brands on Instagram 📈 | 50+ brands · Jaipur → Global | 👇 Free profile audit"*. Lead with a result, not "we handle".
- **Link:** move to a link page or the site (`artboxsocial.com`) with WhatsApp, Portfolio and Book a Call buttons. Right now the WhatsApp-only link got 16 taps in 30 days.
- **Highlights:** Results · Clients · Services & Pricing · Process · Team · Careers.
- **Pinned posts:** (1) the 186K viral Reel, (2) your best case-study carousel, (3) a "what we do and who for" carousel.
- **Keyword DM automation** (ManyChat or Meta's built-in tool): a "GROW" comment or DM sends an audit form and starts a lead conversation.

### Audience cleanup
- Unfollow the inactive and irrelevant accounts among the 1,732 you follow, and bring that number below 500.
- Remove obvious bot followers in batches of about 100 a day (accounts with no profile photo or no posts, from regions you don't serve). Reach as a share of followers will rise.
- Grow local followers on purpose: **Collab posts** with Jaipur brands and clients (you currently have **0 collab posts**, which share reach with both audiences), Jaipur geotags, and features of local cafés and boutiques.

### Stories (currently no data, which suggests they are rarely used)
Post 3 to 5 stories a day: polls ("Which hook would you scroll past?"), work-in-progress clips, client wins, and a weekly "Ask the agency" question box. Stories keep followers seeing you between feed posts.

## 4. 90-day roadmap

| Weeks | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **1 to 2** | Fix the foundation | New bio and link page, highlights, pinned posts, DM automation, start cleaning up who you follow, build a content bank of 12 posts |
| **3 to 6** | Consistency | 4 posts a week (2 Reels, 2 carousels), daily stories, 2 collab posts with clients, 1 case study a week |
| **7 to 10** | Amplify winners | Make 3 new versions of every Reel that beats 3K reach. Boost the best case-study carousel to Jaipur, Delhi and Mumbai business owners aged 25 to 44 (₹300 to ₹500 a day) |
| **11 to 13** | Convert | Launch a "Free Instagram Audit" lead magnet, start a founder-led talking-head series, review KPIs and adjust the pillar mix |
