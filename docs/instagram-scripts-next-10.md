## 10. Reel: trend-jack template (use within 24 hours of a big moment)
**Pillar:** Trend-jack · **Length:** 7–10s · **Goal:** shares

Trend-jacks only work if they're fast. Keep this template ready, and when a big cricket, Bollywood or viral moment happens, shoot it the same day.

**Formula:** *[Famous moment or dialogue]* + *"Marketing version:"* + *[agency or brand-owner pain]*

| Time | Visual | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1s | Team member recreating the moment (pose, dialogue or reaction). | **[The moment everyone's talking about]** |
| 1–5s | Same energy, but in the office. | **Marketing version:** [punchline] |
| 5–8s | Reaction shot from the team. | — |

**Ready-to-use examples:**
- **Cricket win:** *"India chasing 180 in the last over"* → **Marketing version:** "Us at 11:59 PM posting the client's Diwali Reel."
- **Big Bollywood release:** recreate the hero's entry → **Marketing version:** "When the client finally says 'Approved, go live.'"
- **Viral meme format:** use the format → **Marketing version:** "Business owner checking Reel views every 4 minutes."

**Caption template:**
> [Moment] but make it marketing. 😂
>
> Tag someone who gets it.
>
> #[trendinghashtag] #[event] #marketingmemes #agencylife #artboxsocial

**Rule:** if the moment is more than 48 hours old, skip it. A stale trend-jack does worse than not posting.

## Pre-flight checklist (every post)
- [ ] Text visible in the first second, with no logo intro
- [ ] Reel is under 15s (talking head under 30s)
- [ ] Captions or subtitles burned in (most people watch on mute)
- [ ] Pinned "GROW" CTA comment
- [ ] Jaipur geotag
- [ ] Client tagged or invited as a Collab where relevant
- [ ] Shared to Stories within 1 hour, with a poll or question sticker
- [ ] Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes
- [ ] After 48h: note reach, 3-second skip rate, saves and shares. Make 3 new versions of anything that beats 3K reach
