## Platform

web

## Users
Two equally important client groups:
- **Jaipur local businesses**: gyms, schools, furniture and car showrooms, boutiques. Owners who know they need to be on Instagram but have no in-house team.
- **Indian D2C and lifestyle brands**: jewellery, fashion, home and kidswear labels selling online, who need content that looks premium enough to sell.

Both arrive judging one thing: can this team make *my* brand look good on a phone screen, and keep it up every week?

## Product Purpose
Artbox Social is a social media agency based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. The site exists to turn a visiting business owner into an enquiry (contact form, phone or WhatsApp, email).

## Positioning
The brand line is **"Everything Artsy"**: Artbox is a creative-first studio, not a performance shop. Lead offer, in order:
1. **Content & reels**: shoots, reels, visuals and copy.
2. **Social media management**: calendar, posting, community, reporting.
3. **Social media strategy**: positioning, content pillars, planning.

Ads, influencer work, websites, SEO, branding and photography are supporting services.
